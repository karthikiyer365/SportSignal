Big number and colour = value per 100 actions the team started there: how dangerous a typical touch in that zone was (quality). Every on-ball action the player made all season that moved his team's goal odds by at least ±0.025, team attacking left to right, each line running from where the action started to where it ended. Colour = kind of action: shooting · passing · carrying · defending · goalkeeping. Line thickness runs from his most costly action (thinnest) to his most valuable (thickest). Squad list: click a player to draw his season. Value = sum over all his actions; per 90 below 900 minutes is greyed out because it swings on a few games.
